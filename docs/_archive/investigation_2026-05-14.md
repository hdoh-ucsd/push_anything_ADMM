# Investigation 2026-05-14 — deck failures under post-F3 regime

## Executive summary

- **Deck's three failures do NOT all reproduce.** Under post-F3 + watchdog=50 forcing 8 c3-mode entries on Path D, the deck's η° magnitude collapsed (−157.6 → peak 0.42), the wrong-direction box motion collapsed (−105 mm → ≤1 mm net per dwell, ~12 mm SW total over 8 s), and only torque saturation partially survived (joint-2 clamped at 30 Nm in every entry, ‖u‖ ≈ 40 Nm).
- **Manipulability is NOT the binding constraint.** All 8 watchdog-forced entries land at the *same* EE pose (≈ (−0.062, +0.097, 0.025)), with κ(J) = 5.30–5.33 and Yoshikawa w(q) = 0.143. The arm is in a well-conditioned, dexterous configuration. Deck Pattern 1 (high κ co-occurs with all three failures) is unsupported.
- **Pattern 2 — regime-shifted — matches.** The post-F3 regime exposes a different binding constraint than the deck's framing: the kIK guide-path's stable fixed point (9.4.4 finding) parks the EE 13.4 cm short of every chosen sample target. Whether the wrapper picks `strat_0` (452/801, 56%) or `prev_repos` (338/801, 42%), the EE does not advance, so c3-mode (1-step dwell, forced) lands on the same non-contact-pushing pose every time.
- **Binding constraint is Effect A (kIK reachability of sample targets).** Cross-checks Item 2.1 G1–G4. The watchdog-as-instrument is what surfaced this: with watchdog disabled, c3-mode never fires at all (deck observation: 0/801 entries because best_other/c_curr ≤ 0.96, well below 1.30). With watchdog forcing entries, c3-mode lands on a useless EE pose because the planner cannot move the EE off the fixed point.
- **Recommended fix surface (low-risk first move):** kIK guide-path's initial-knot recipe in `control/sampling_c3/reposition_ik.py:_build_guide_path` (lines 873–906) — replace `p_next = next_waypoint(p_now=p_curr, …)` with a path that does not rebuild from `ee_now` every loop. Moderate scope. Alternatives enumerated in Phase C.

## Phase B — diagnosis

**Pattern 2 — regime-shifted.** The deck's binding constraint (the F = J⁻ᵀ τ architectural gap) is not what is binding under post-F3. The manipulability data refutes Pattern 1: κ(J)=5.30–5.33 is well-conditioned, w(q)=0.143 is comfortably in the dexterous workspace, yet two of three deck failures evaporate and the third (saturation) survives in form but not in consequence — joint-2 still clamps at 30 Nm but the box does not move. The binding constraint is upstream of the C3 inner solver entirely: the kIK guide-path produces a stable fixed point at EE ≈ (−0.062, +0.097, 0.025), 13.4 cm from `prev_repos` (per [GS-tgt] line at the final step) and ~13 cm from `strat_0`. The watchdog forces c3 every 50 free-mode steps as configured, but because the EE never moves off the fixed point, every c3 entry sees the same (q, x0, J), the same η° band, and the same uselessly-aimed commanded torque. The 1-step c3 dwell then exits via `kToReposCost` (c_curr ≈ 116k ≫ c_repos ≈ 64k, gap far exceeds the 30% relative hysteresis), and the cycle repeats. This is the persistence mechanism flagged in journal 2026-05-13 ("Persistence … is the next binding constraint, surfaced first by 9.4.7 Option A and now confirmed standalone post-F3"), with one new observation: the persistence failure is *consequence*, not *cause* — c3-mode persistence is short because there is nothing for c3 to grip on at the fixed-point pose, and that pose is determined by the kIK planner, not by C3.

**Recommended fix surface — Effect A, kIK guide-path (`control/sampling_c3/reposition_ik.py:_build_guide_path`, lines 873–906).** This is the same locus identified by 9.4.4 ("`p_guide[:, 0] = next_waypoint(ee_now, p_target, ds=0.01 m)` is rebuilt from the current `ee_now` every loop, producing a stable fixed point"). The fix is targeted, localized to ~30 LOC, and tests the binding-constraint hypothesis directly: if knot-0 is anchored to `ee_now + ds·(p_target − ee_now)/‖·‖` independent of the prior loop's EE drift (or, more aggressively, the path is constructed once per repos-target and replayed), the EE should march off the fixed point; if the box still doesn't move once EE actually reaches a contact pose, then a different mechanism is binding. This is preferable to (a) deck Track B-1 (F-decision-variable LCS — months of work, premised on architectural gap not supported by current data); (b) deck Track B-2 (OSC — requires B-1 first); (c) Track A sequenced planner (larger scope; subsumes the kIK fix); (d) wrapper entry-gate tuning (lower 1.30 threshold — orthogonal to the binding constraint).

**Cross-check against Item 2.1 Effects A/B/C.** The binding constraint here is **Effect A** (planner / kIK reachability of sample targets), surfaced previously by the 9.4 → 9.4.4 chain and re-confirmed post-F3 by the current data: strat_0 wins 56% of sample arbitration (Effect C subdued by F3) but kIK still cannot move EE to strat_0 (Effect A binding). Effect B (executor home-hold, joint-2 clamping at 30 Nm) is present but is a downstream consequence of the kIK fixed point — the integrator winds up because the PD target is changing every loop while the EE is pinned. No new effect needed; the post-F3 regime cleanly puts A ahead of B ahead of C in binding order.

## Phase C — fix sketch

**Recommended fix — kIK initial-knot anchoring.** Files: `control/sampling_c3/reposition_ik.py:873–906` (`_build_guide_path`) and possibly `control/sampling_c3/reposition.py` (helper `next_waypoint`, line ~50). Scope: ~30–60 LOC, moderate complexity. The current loop rebuilds `p_guide` from `ee_now` every wrapper invocation, so the first knot is always one `ds = speed·dt = 0.20·0.05 = 0.01 m` step ahead of *wherever the EE happens to be* — and where the EE happens to be is the closed-loop fixed point of the joint-PD law against the prior loop's guide path, identified analytically in 9.4.3 (`Kp·|q_err| + Ki·I_max = tau_g(q_now) + tau_g(q_target)` is satisfied with non-zero q_err). The fix is to cache the guide path per `p_target` (or per "repos target identity") and replay it knot-by-knot, only re-planning when the wrapper switches to a new `target_label`. Approximate change shape:

```
# In SamplingC3MPC._build_samples → _select_target →
# RepositionIK.compute_torque(plant_ctx, p_target=p_repos, …):
# Cache (p_target_hash, p_guide) on the tracker; reuse for as long as the
# wrapper's p_repos is unchanged (target_changed=N in [GS-tgt] log). On
# target_changed=Y, rebuild from current ee_now. Knot index advances by 1
# per control loop until the path is consumed.
```

Risks: (a) `target_changed=N` is currently emitted on >99% of steps in the Phase A run, so cache hit rate would be near-100% — invariant well-exercised; (b) IK feasibility along the cached path is already validated knot-by-knot, so caching does not bypass the existing feasibility gate; (c) the existing `last_knot0_feasible` poison mechanism (`wrapper.py:518–`) continues to work as designed since per-knot IK still runs. Tests potentially affected: `tests/test_reposition_ik_*.py` if any pin the per-loop guide-rebuild behaviour — needs inspection before changing. Estimated wall-clock: **0.5–1.5 days** to implement + validate against the Phase A measurement (re-run with the cached-guide variant; check that strat_0-driven c3 entries land at *different* EE poses, that κ(J) varies across entries, and that the box moves ≥ a few cm).

**Alternative 1 — wrapper "watchdog-with-persistence" lockout.** Files: `control/sampling_c3/wrapper.py:432–442` (`kForceC3Watchdog` block) + `mode_switch.py` to add a "stay in c3 for N steps" override. Scope: ~20 LOC, trivial. Risk: bypasses the binding constraint (kIK fixed point) — c3 would persist longer, but the EE pose is still pinned, so c3 may simply commit useless torque for 30 steps instead of 1. Empirically tests whether c3's planned λ can move the box from the fixed-point pose; if yes, kIK is downstream and the persistence fix is sufficient; if no, kIK is binding and we still need Alternative 0. **0.25 days.**

**Alternative 2 — Track A sequenced planner / deck framing.** Files: extensive — new module under `control/sampling_c3/` plus surgery in `wrapper.py`. Scope: major (likely 2–4 weeks). Risk: rewrites the wrapper's sample-selection-and-execute architecture; correctness validation requires re-running the full F2/F3 verdict-A suite + new tests. Subsumes the kIK fix as a special case. Not justified until the localized kIK fix is shown insufficient.

**Alternative 3 — `c_sample` rebalance to suppress prev_repos lock entirely.** Files: `control/sampling_c3/inner_solve.py` (cost weights `w_align`, `w_travel`) and possibly the buffer-retention thresholds. Scope: ~10 LOC, but moderate validation cost (the post-F3 caveat in `docs/paper_alignment_plan.md:72` notes 1a/1b/1c rationales must be re-derived against post-F3 numbers, which haven't been done). Risk: 9.4.5-D direct-arithmetic falsification argued this is mathematically dominated; that argument needs re-checking against today's c_sample histogram (prev_repos 338, strat_0 452) before being treated as falsified again. **1–2 days for re-characterization and tuning.**

## Open questions

- **Is the kIK-guide cache strictly safe under floating-base object motion?** When the wrapper switches `p_target` due to a moving object, the cached guide path is stale. Current invocation already supplies a new `p_target` per loop; caching keyed on `target_changed=Y` (already-emitted boolean) should suffice, but a cross-check against `tests/test_reposition_ik_*` would confirm no test pins the every-loop-rebuild contract.
- **If the kIK fix moves the EE, does the C3 inner solver then move the box?** This investigation cannot answer that — every observed entry is at the fixed point. The recommended fix is also the cheapest experiment to answer it. If post-fix c3-entries continue to fail to move the box (Δobj·ĝ ≈ 0 across varying EE poses, varying κ(J)), then the deck's architectural framing partially returns to relevance — but only conditional on first ruling out the planner-level confound.
- **Does the deck's `prepositioned` regime show η° = +0.43 today, or has it also collapsed?** The investigation ran the default (non-prepositioned) Path D. A one-shot `--prepositioned` rerun would re-anchor the deck's η° comparison; without it the η° interpretation rests on the default-IK column only.
- **Is the joint-2 30 Nm clamp Effect B (integrator) or just gravity?** The Phase A data shows the clamp at every entry, but does not isolate which mechanism dominates. Item 2.1's Effect B framing assigned it to integrator authority; reconfirming this under post-F3 (one targeted probe of `tau_g(q_now)` at the fixed-point q vs the integrator's contribution) would close the question.
