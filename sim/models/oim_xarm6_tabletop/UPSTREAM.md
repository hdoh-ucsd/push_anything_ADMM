# OIM xArm6 tabletop assets

Imported from NikolaRaicevic2001/Object-Informed-Manipulation-MJX.
The five requested MJCF scenes and their OBJ assets are unchanged.
The seven xArm STL meshes are converted to OBJ because Drake's MJCF
parser does not accept STL; xarm6.xml changes only those extensions.
The source oim/configs/robots/xarm6.yaml is vendored unchanged and
is the wrapper's controller, run, and evaluation configuration.
