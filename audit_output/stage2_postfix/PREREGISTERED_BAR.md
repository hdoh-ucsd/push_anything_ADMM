**Baseline:** `38dbf18` (pre-fix), 0/20 seeds formed EE-box contact.

## Bar

**PASS** = EE-box contact forms on **≥ 15 / 20 seeds** under the canonical sweep
(`scripts/run_canonical_baseline_sweep.sh` — task-id 4, --solver c3plus, --sampling-c3,
--admm-iter 25, --max-time 2.5s, --seed {0..19}, --no-record).

**"Contact forms"** = at least one `[STEP]` line with `contact=Y` in the seed's run log.
This is the same criterion used for the 0/20 baseline measurement.

## Why ≥15 (and not weaker)

A 3/20 result would be a reshuffled lucky-draw distribution, not a fix — that's the
seed-sensitivity trap captured in `feedback_baseline_substrate.md`. A clear majority
threshold (≥15/20) requires the fix to be reproducibly contact-forming across the
seed distribution, not just shift the random odds.

## What this DOES NOT measure (do not collapse)

- Box motion / goal progress. Contact rate is the immediate kinematic check that
  the EE actually reaches the box's contact surface. Sustained productive push,
  contact direction quality, ADMM convergence, and goal distance are SEPARATE
  measurements deferred to later stages.
- Stage-2 PASS unblocks testing the next layer (face-picker stash@{0} under a
  descending arm; SC3 mis-direction/recoil statistics that were n=1; ADMM
  non-convergence at 25/25 iters). It does NOT equal "pushing works."

## Failure modes the sweep will discriminate

| outcome | reading |
|---|---|
| ≥15/20 contact | PASS — fix unblocked the substrate; next blocker is downstream of contact formation |
| 8–14/20 contact | PARTIAL — fix produced meaningful improvement but reveals secondary contact-formation issue (likely the directional face-picker still picking TOP face on some seeds; revisit stash@{0}) |
| 1–7/20 contact | FAIL — fix moved the needle but the contact-formation pipeline has a deeper issue (e.g., the dispatcher still doesn't reach descent in most cases; investigate per-seed mode distribution) |
| 0/20 contact | UNEXPECTED — re-examine fix; likely a new failure mode introduced (e.g., the arm descended past the box, or some other regression) |

## Reposition path verification (the user's #2 check, settled before run)

The post-fix descent in seed 0 was generated by the c3-mode OSC tracking the
Lever-3 downward setpoint (56/60 ticks), not by the reposition tracker. The
reposition path was exercised — `traj_type=kPiecewiseLinear` per the run log
fingers `PiecewiseLinearTracker` as the active tracker, and `wrapper.py:1304`
calls `self.tracker.compute_torque(...)` every tick. But the wrapper then
overwrites the tracker's `u` with the OSC's `u_imp` at `wrapper.py:1608`, so
PWL's `u_pd` never reaches the plant in the SamplingC3MPC + OSC flow.

Consequence: the "under-comp → arm sags" failure mode the user flagged
cannot manifest via PWL in this configuration, because PWL's `u` is bypassed.
Mathematical invariant still holds for any future standalone-PWL caller: pre-fix
PWL emitted `tau_g + u_pd`; post-fix PWL emits `u_pd` and the main loop now adds
`tau_g`. Same total torque at the plant input.

The 4 free-mode ticks in seed 0 (steps 13/26/39/52) showed tracking error 2 mm
pre and 2 mm post — confirming the OSC's free-mode tracking (which is what
actually fires in free mode) is preserved.
